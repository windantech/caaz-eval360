# CAAZ Performance360 — Eval360 demonstration for the Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe

A demonstration of Eval360 configured as CAAZ's aviation safety, compliance and performance
management system: the State Safety Programme, safety indicators, ICAO compliance, regulatory
oversight (inspections, findings, corrective actions, verification), air navigation and
infrastructure, ATAZ training, strategy and executive reporting.

**Public CAAZ information** (functions, mandate, 2026 priorities, regulatory domains, commissioned
systems, ATAZ status, air service agreements) is drawn from caaz.co.zw, checked on 6 October 2026,
and marked with its source. **Inspection, safety, compliance and performance records** are synthetic,
use fictional organisations and aircraft registrations, and are marked **Demo Data**.

Eval360 is the performance, compliance and management layer. It does not replace ATC, radar,
flight-planning, registry, licensing or other operational aviation systems.

## Open it

Open `index.html` (or `caaz_eval360_demo.html`) in a browser — from disk or any static server. It opens
on the Executive Dashboard as the Director General; switch to any of the fourteen demo roles from the
name in the top bar. For a presentation start at **Overview → Demo Walkthrough**.

## Check it

```bash
bash scripts/selftest.sh
```
